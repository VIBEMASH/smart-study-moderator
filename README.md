# 🧠 Smart Study Moderator  
**AI-powered focus for group study**

## 📌 Problem Statement
Remote group study sessions often lose focus due to distractions, off-topic conversations, and lack of engagement tracking. Students struggle to collaborate effectively, maintain discipline, and identify weak areas during online discussions.

## 💡 Solution
Smart Study Moderator is an AI-powered collaborative study platform where students can create or join study rooms and engage in focused discussions. An AI moderator ethically listens to conversations, tracks topic adherence, detects probable disengagement, and supports learning in real time—without violating privacy.

---

## 🚀 App Flow

### Initial Screen
- **App Name:** Smart Study Moderator  
- **Tagline:** *AI-powered focus for group study*

### Primary Actions
- 🟦 Create Study Room  
- 🟩 Join Study Room  

---

## 🏠 Study Room Creation & Joining

### Create Study Room
- Generate a unique room ID / room code  
- Associate room with:
  - study topic
  - session host
  - start time  
- Creator becomes the session host

### Share Room Code
- One-click copy option  
- Native share options:
  - WhatsApp
  - shareable link
  - system share sheet (mobile & desktop)

### Join Study Room
- Join using a valid room code  
- View:
  - study topic
  - active participants  
- Join options:
  - microphone ON/OFF
  - text-only mode  

---

## 🔊 Voice & Text-Based Discussion

### Speech-to-Text (STT)
- Students speak via microphone
- AI converts speech to live text
- Transcript displayed at the bottom
- No raw audio stored

### Text-to-Speech (TTS)
- AI replies via text or optional voice
- Voice tone:
  - calm
  - supportive
  - non-authoritative

---

## 🤖 AI Core Features

### Topic Adherence Detection
- Semantic analysis of discussion
- Classifies messages as:
  - on-topic
  - partially related
  - off-topic
- Polite, minimal AI nudges when needed

Example:
> “Let’s refocus on the deadlock conditions we were discussing.”

---

### Sensor-Based Presence Detection (Privacy-Safe)

**Laptop / Desktop**
- browser tab visibility
- window focus
- keyboard & mouse inactivity

**Mobile**
- app background state
- screen lock
- touch inactivity
- optional motion inactivity (opt-in)

**Logic**
- Uses multiple signals
- Detects probable disengagement
- Sends private, supportive nudges only

---

### Privacy & Consent
- No camera, GPS, or biometric tracking
- No permanent audio recording
- Analysis only during active sessions
- Opt-in for advanced detection
- Users can mute AI or switch to text-only anytime

---

### Engagement Scoring
- Lightweight, ethical engagement score
- Based on participation consistency
- Used only for personalization
- No ranking or public comparison

---

### Study Support
- Smart study schedule creation
- Break monitoring with healthy reminders
- Performance analytics:
  - strong topics
  - weak areas
  - misconceptions

---

### Ask AI Anytime
- Voice or text queries:
  - “AI, explain this”
  - “Summarize the discussion”
- Concise, topic-aligned responses
